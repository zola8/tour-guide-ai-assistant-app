ETH Zurich (Swiss Federal Institute of Technology Zurich) is a public research university in Zurich, Switzerland, specializing in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM). Founded in 1854–1855 as part of the Swiss Federal Institutes of Technology Domain, it is consistently ranked among the top universities globally.

Key Facts & Figures

• Students & Staff: Around 26,900 students (including over 4,200 doctoral students) and more than 550 professors.
• Campuses: Main downtown campus (Zentrum), the Hönggerberg campus ("Science City") on the outskirts of Zurich, and facilities in Basel.
• Languages: Bachelor's programs are primarily taught in German, while most Master's and doctoral programs are taught in English.
• Notable Affiliations: Linked to 22 Nobel laureates—including Albert Einstein—as well as Fields Medalists, Pritzker Prize winners, and a Turing Award winner.
• Innovation: Over 500 spin-off companies have emerged from its research, supported by strong ties to industry and government.

ETH Zurich (Swiss Federal Institute of Technology Zurich) is famous as one of the world’s top universities for science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM), renowned for its Nobel Prize history, Albert Einstein connection, and cutting-edge research.
• Albert Einstein & Nobel Laureates: Einstein studied at ETH Zurich (graduating in 1900) and later taught there as a professor. More than 20 Nobel laureates are affiliated with the university as alumni, professors, or researchers, alongside Fields Medalists, Pritzker Prize winners, and Turing Award winners.
• Global STEM Rankings: Founded in 1855, ETH Zurich consistently ranks among the top universities globally and in continental Europe—especially in engineering, computer science, earth sciences, and natural sciences.
• Research & Innovation: It drives fundamental research and practical industry spin-offs, generating dozens of patents and spin-off companies annually while partnering with major tech firms and research institutions.
• Iconic Campus & Views: Its neoclassical Main Building in Zurich (designed by Gottfried Semper) and the Polyterrasse viewing deck offer panoramic views over the city, Lake Zurich, and the Alps, accessible via the Polybahn funicular.

Tours & Practical Tips

• Guided Tours: Book a 60-minute student-led meet ETH Tour (CHF 10 per person) covering either the Zentrum Main Building (including rooftop access) or Hönggerberg.
• Self-Guided Audio & Flyers: Download the free ETH-to-go brochure or use ETH's digital audio guides to walk in Albert Einstein’s footsteps at your own pace.
• Public Transit: From Thalwil, take an S-Bahn train to Zürich HB, then hop on Tram 6 or 10 to ETH/Universitätsspital or take the Polybahn from Central (covered by ZVV Zone 110).

To researchers, ETH offers an inspiring working environment, to students, a comprehensive education.

ETH Zurich trains true experts and prepares its students to carry out their tasks as critical members of their communities, making an important contribution to the sustainable development of science, the economy and society.

Master’s degree studies
Is it your goal to do cutting-edge research, start your own company or pursue a career in business, industry or administration? A Master’s degree from ETH Zurich would be the ideal stepping stone to your future.

Beginning your studies
ETH Zurich is one of the world’s leading technical and scientific universities. With members from over 100 nations, we offer our students an inspiring, diverse environment.

During your studies you will have the chance to interact with top researchers, address the latest scientific knowledge in your field and apply it in practice.

Personal initiative and cross-disciplinary competences
Take advantage of the range of selections offered in our curricula and shape your studies according to your personal strengths and interests. We count on your personal initiative, and will help you to develop your own projects. Besides imparting knowledge and skills for your future career we will prepare you for the responsibilities you will assume in society as a graduate. For this reason, we lay great weight on the development of cross-disciplinary competences such as critical reflection and argumentation.

The prerequisites for undertaking graduate studies at ETH Zurich are a Bachelor’s degree from an internationally recognised university and excellent grades. Our students immerse themselves independently and enthusiastically in their chosen fields, pursue discussion with peers and faculty, and value cultural and academic diversity.
